# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

Praxis is an open project that depends on a welcoming, professional, and
collaborative community. This Code of Conduct sets the standard for how everyone
who participates — contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and issue reporters — is
expected to treat one another, so that the project stays a productive and respectful
place to work.

## Our standard

We expect everyone in this community to:

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive in all interactions.
- Welcome newcomers and assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully, focusing on the work rather than the person.
- Respect differing viewpoints, experiences, and technical opinions.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Keep discussion focused on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Behavior that makes the community unwelcoming or unsafe is not tolerated. This
includes, but is not limited to:

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Discrimination or exclusionary jokes or language.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or community spaces.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions, and any other official channel — and also applies when an
individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the
project maintainer at **jwillz7667@gmail.com**. Reports will be handled
confidentially. Please include what happened, where, when, and any context or links
that help us understand the situation.

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and safety of anyone who reports an
incident in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this standard. They may take
any action they deem appropriate, proportionate to the situation, including:

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning explaining the issue.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary restriction** — a time-limited block from interaction in project spaces.
4. **Permanent ban** — removal from the community for severe or repeated violations.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face
repercussions as determined by the project's leadership.
